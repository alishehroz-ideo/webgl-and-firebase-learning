# Art Credits

- **Sticker items** (camel, palm, boat, turtle, crab, shell, fish, wave, tent, cactus, sun, star, bird, flower):
  [OpenMoji](https://openmoji.org) — the open-source emoji library. Licensed **CC BY-SA 4.0**.
- **Backgrounds** (desert & sea landscapes): flat vector illustrations generated for this project.

Assets are hosted on Firebase Hosting (`/assets/…`) and referenced by URL in the Realtime Database
`assetCatalog`, so art can be added/swapped with no app rebuild.
